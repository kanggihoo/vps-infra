Nightshift — your assistant works while you sleep

Nightshift is a personal AI assistant built on Claude. It keeps working overnight and has a clear overview ready when your day starts.

  • Always on

    Runs continuously on our own infrastructure, following up on tasks and sources while you're offline.

  • Morning briefing

    Starts each day with a concise overview: what changed, what needs attention, what's on the schedule.

  • Personalized information

    Learns what you care about and surfaces the information that matters to you, not everything.
